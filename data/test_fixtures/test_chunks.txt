Example 1 (COMPLIANT)

The Data Fiduciary provides notice before collecting personal data. Users may withdraw consent at any time. A grievance redressal mechanism is available.

Example 2 (NON-COMPLIANT)

Users cannot withdraw consent once provided. Personal data will be stored indefinitely.

Example 3 (NON-COMPLIANT)

Personal data may be shared with third parties without notice or consent.

Example 4 (COMPLIANT)

Personal data is deleted once the purpose for processing is completed.

Example 5 (NON-COMPLIANT)

No security safeguards are implemented to protect stored personal data.
